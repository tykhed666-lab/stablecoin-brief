# Metrics

Even with 5 testers, define the same numbers you would watch after launch.

| Metric | Definition | How you collect now | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task completion | User gets a sourced conclusion in 10 minutes | Watch 5 sessions | ≥4/5 |
| Treated-as-advice rate | Says they should buy/sell after reading | Ask the fixed question | Drop after v2 |
| Human edit rate | Share of model sentences you rewrote | Your own memo drafts | Track, no vanity target |
| Unsourced count | Statements in Facts with no URL | Count in output | 0 unmarked |

Do not add MAU or "engagement". Those are not decision metrics for this tool.
